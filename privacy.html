SpeakCoach privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. Applies to the SpeakCoach closed-test app (version 0.1.0).

Summary

This version of SpeakCoach works entirely on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your recordings or your results to us or to anyone else.

What the app uses

  • Microphone. When you tap record, the app listens through your microphone to measure things like pace, pauses and loudness, and to work out your practice result. The recording is processed on your phone only. The recording file is deleted from your phone as soon as your result is ready.
  • Practice history. Your name or nickname, goal, results and progress are stored on your phone only, so you can see your history. We cannot see them.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, upload, sell or share your voice, your results or any personal information in this version.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics tools.
  • We do not use your recordings to train any model.

Your choices

In Settings, Delete all my data erases your history and profile from the phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Children

SpeakCoach is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Future versions

If a later version adds optional cloud analysis, we will ask for your clear permission first and update this policy before it is released.
